## Payment workflow

1. Confirm the project scope, price, currency, deadline, and payment milestones in a private written agreement.
2. Send the client this payment information privately as part of the agreed invoice or payment request.
3. For a first payment, consider a small test transfer only if both parties agree on the amount and fees in advance.
4. Ask the client for the transaction ID (TXID), then independently verify the transaction and token on the TRON blockchain explorer.
5. Treat payment as received only after the correct USDT token transfer to the exact address is confirmed on-chain. A screenshot or a wallet notification alone is not proof of payment.
6. Deliver the agreed work and issue a receipt stating the service, amount, date, and transaction reference.
