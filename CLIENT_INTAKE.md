## Test access

- Prefer a dedicated test account with minimum permissions.
- Do not send production passwords, payment credentials, private keys, seed phrases, or real customer data.
- If login is required, agree on a secure temporary access method before testing.

- Requested service:

- Agreed price and currency:

Testing begins only after the scope, authorization, price, and delivery expectations are agreed in writing.
